Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Rclone Google Drive is a personal application used by its owner to connect a privately operated home server to Google Drive.

Data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files, folders, file metadata, and sharing permissions as required to perform user-initiated file migration, upload, and sharing operations.

How data is used

Google Drive data is used only to carry out operations requested by the application owner, such as listing files, copying files, creating folders, and sharing selected files with specific recipients.

Data storage

OAuth credentials may be stored locally on the owner's private server. The application does not operate a separate public database for Google user data.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Files are shared with third parties only when the application owner explicitly chooses to share them through Google Drive.

Data retention

Files copied to Google Drive remain there until removed by the Google account owner. Local OAuth authorization can be revoked at any time through the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

This application is operated for personal use only. Questions regarding this policy may be directed to the application owner through the contact information associated with the Google OAuth consent screen.
